Internet

  We need at least 500 Mbps down and 20 Mbps up — real speeds, not "up to".

- We're fine with autopay and paperless billing.

Phones
- Keep all three lines on one account and keep our numbers.
- My line (A) is my hotspot when I'm at the café — make sure hotspot covers what I actually use.
- Arjun (line B) visits family in Mexico twice a year, about 5 days each trip. His phone has to work there.
- Meera (line C) barely uses data.

General
- Compare over two years, not just the teaser price.
- Don't give our names, numbers, emails or address to any company or website while you shop.
  San Jose / 951 is fine for checking what's available.
