# Worked example (not a real offer)

Offer: $70/mo regular, promo $35/mo for 6 months, autopay −$5/mo, equipment $10/mo, a $3 monthly fee,
one-time $50 install. Household accepts autopay.

- Months 1–6:  35 − 5 + 10 + 3 = 43.00  → 6 × 43.00 = 258.00
- Months 7–12: 70 − 5 + 10 + 3 = 78.00  → 6 × 78.00 = 468.00
- 12-month cost = 258.00 + 468.00 + 50.00 = **776.00**
- Months 13–24: 12 × 78.00 = 936.00
- 24-month cost = 776.00 + 936.00 = **1,712.00**

Mobile day-pass example: $8/day, one line travels 6 days a year → 24-month passes = 8 × 6 × 2 = $96.00.
